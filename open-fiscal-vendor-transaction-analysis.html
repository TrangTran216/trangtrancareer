Project 1 · Microsoft Excel

Open FI$Cal Vendor Transaction Analysis

An Excel-based portfolio project that structures and reviews public vendor-transaction activity for Business Unit 509, Business & Economic Development.

Download workbookDownload resume
4,083public transaction records reviewed
1-9fiscal periods represented
$418.4Mnet transaction activity in scope

Objective

Make a complex public-data file easier to review.

I transformed the vendor-transaction dataset into a review-ready workbook that supports data-quality checks, fiscal-period summaries, and PivotTable-based exploration. The project demonstrates a careful, documented approach to data handling for administrative and financial review.

The source scope is limited to public Open FI$Cal vendor-transaction activity for the specified Business Unit and department. It is not a representation of all California spending.

Process

How the workbook was built

  1. Structure and standardize.Reviewed fields and built helper columns for fiscal periods, positive and negative amounts, privacy flags, and quality checks.
  2. Validate and investigate.Applied formulas, lookups, filters, and review flags to assess required fields, accounting-date and fiscal-period logic, duplicate-looking records, and negative adjustments.
  3. Summarize and explain.Created fiscal metrics and PivotTable views for trends, account categories, funds, programs, vendors, confidential rows, negative adjustments, and document review.
  4. Document the logic.Added clear formulas, review guidance, and supporting details so the workbook can be checked, updated, and explained to a nontechnical reviewer.

Key findings

Clear scope, transparent handling.

The project emphasizes accurate presentation and review of the available public records—not unsupported conclusions about broader state spending.

Fiscal coverage

Accounting dates run from July 1, 2025 to March 30, 2026, covering fiscal periods 1 through 9.

Program concentration

Within this dataset, Small Business Expansion represents the largest share of net activity, followed by Go-Biz and other identified programs.

Privacy-aware analysis

Masked or confidential vendor rows remain in relevant totals, are summarized separately, and are excluded from named-vendor identification.

Skills demonstrated

Excel, data validation, quality checks, PivotTables, fiscal reporting, and documentation.
